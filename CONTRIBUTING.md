# Contributing

Bug reports and ideas are welcome as GitHub issues.

This repository holds the addon and its built-in plans; the planner that builds the plans is not public, so a
pull request can change the addon, not the plans themselves (a plan that looks wrong is best reported as an issue,
with `/bee report`).

Pull requests are welcome too, on one condition. By opening a pull request you agree that:

1. the contribution is your own work, and you have the right to give it;
2. it is licensed to the public under this project's licenses (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)); and
3. you also grant noopz a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, change, sublicense and
   relicense your contribution under any terms, including commercial ones, so the project can change its licensing
   later without having to track down every contributor.

You keep the copyright in your contribution and you'll be credited for it. If you can't agree to this, please open an
issue describing the change instead.
